Hyper Roll

  • Buy Digimon from the shop and drag them onto your half of the board. Your level is how many Digimon you can field.
  • Three copies digivolve. 3 Agumon become a Greymon, 3 Greymon become a MetalGreymon.
  • Items sit on the pedestals left of the board. Drag one onto a Digimon. Two components make a completed item.
  • Your Little Legend walks where you right-click. Walk it into loot orbs to open them.
