# Set 2: Dragon Realm

Adapts: TFT Set 7: Dragonlands (58 champions) plus 2 Set 7.5 Uncharted Realms slots (Zippy, Graves).

The Mega dragons of the Digital World (Breakdramon, Goldramon, Megidramon, Examon, Ouryumon, Slayerdramon) with the X-Antibody knights, the Olympos gods and the ancient stone guardians. Every form uses a Cyber Sleuth, Digimon Links, Next Order, Re:Digitize or Time Stranger model. TFT Set 7 was built around dragon units that take two slots; Digimon has more dragon Megas than any other kind.

60 shop lines (by cost: 1-cost 13, 2-cost 13, 3-cost 14, 4-cost 12, 5-cost 8). 49 have animated models for all three forms. Estimated new downloads: 112 files, about 235.1 MB.

## Set mechanic

**Mega Dragons** (adapts Dragon units and the Treasure Dragon (TFT Set 7))

- Dragon units (the eight lines with the Dragon trait) take 2 team slots, add +3 to the trait marked on them and gain +700 max HP. The bonus only works with exactly one Dragon on the board.
- Dragon lines cost 4, 5 or 6 gold in the shop (TFT 7.5 used 6/7/8 for 3/4/5-cost dragons; this keeps them one gold above their tier). A Dragon can be bought only once per player.
- Treasure Dragon: the stage 4 wild round (4-4) is a Treasure Goldramon. Beating it offers a choice of 4 loot orbs with one free reroll; at least 5 components have dropped before it.
- Three copies still digivolve. A Dragon line reaching its final form keeps the 2-slot cost.

Engine work: Board size counts slots instead of units, a per-unit marked trait worth +3, and a once-per-player purchase flag. The Treasure Goldramon reuses this set's Goldramon model at 1.5x scale.
