## Set mechanics

- Dragon units: 4- and 5-cost Dragons need 2 team slots, add +3 to their marked trait, and get bonus health; the bonus works only with exactly 1 Dragon fielded (Dragons were unique). Shop price of Set 7 Dragons: unknown in sources used (7.5 changed it to 6/7/8 gold). [CDragon trait text, 12.17 notes]
- Treasure Dragon replaced Raptors at 4-7: a PvE reward that lets you pick or reroll item/loot offers. Minimum 5 components from early PvE, so 9 components including carousels before the Treasure Dragon. [12.11 notes]
- Augments moved to 2-1, 3-2, 4-2; one augment reroll per game; Gold tier became the standard offer. Crown and Soul augments give 1 emblem/unit, a unit in the trait, and a key item. [12.11 notes]
- Shimmerscale trait gives special Shimmerscale items (Crown of Champions, Determined Investor/Diamond Hands, Draven's Axe, Gambler's Blade, Goldmancer's Staff, Mogul's Mail, Needlessly Big Gem); they are removed when the unit goes to the bench. [wiki Shimmerscale]
- Neeko's Help renamed Champion Duplicator. PvE gold coins auto-collect. Stage 5-6 PvE drops emblems only for active traits, and can drop uncraftable emblems. [12.11 notes]
- Ornn artifacts (Portable Forge items) and Radiant items available through augments. [12.11 notes]

## Economy and system changes

- Augment tier moves for the set, e.g. Ancient Archives I Silver to Gold, Binary Airdrop Gold to Prismatic. [12.11 notes]
- Item changes: Bloodthirster and Gunblade use Omnivamp; Dragon's Claw reworked to 120 MR plus 1.2% max HP regen per enemy targeting the holder every 2 s (20% more on Dragons); Sunfire Cape 400 HP, 1% burn. [12.11 notes]
