## Set mechanics

- Radiant Items replaced Shadow Items: stronger versions of regular items, one chosen from five at the 3-6 Armory. [11.15 notes]
- Divine Blessing: drops once when your Little Legend falls below 40 health; all players get the same essential contents (components, gold, consumables, Spatula, champions). [11.15 notes]
- Tome of Traits: consumable; sell it to open a shop of four emblems, including emblems for traits a Spatula cannot build. [11.15 notes]

## Economy and system changes

- Armory schedule: 2-2 two components; 3-6 five Radiant items; 4-2 guaranteed (2 components, 3 components, or 3 components plus a special item); 5-2 less likely and never components. [11.15 notes]
- Loot orbs: small orb average value 2.54 to 3 gold; minimum after 4-7 is 10 components + 1 Radiant item. [11.15 notes]
- Stage 5+ carousels can contain uncraftable emblems; stage 6+ carousels can be all full items. [11.15 notes]
- Champion count 58 to 57. [11.15 notes]
