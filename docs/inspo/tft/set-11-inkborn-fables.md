## Set mechanics and system changes

- Encounters: random story events that happen 2 to 5 times per game. An Encounter can appear on your board before a PvP round, during a carousel, or move the lobby to an extra carousel. Effects range from gifts to gambles to rule changes for the game. 109 distinct Encounter rows below.
- Opening encounter: the first Encounter happens at the start of the game and sets a lobby-wide rule (for example, all augments a given tier).
- Storyweaver summons and evolves a hero named Kayle; Fortune gains Luck from lost player combats and rolls a die at each Festival; Lovers (Rakan and Xayah) swaps which Lover is fielded based on front or back rows; Fated lets the player pair two Fated units for a pair bonus; Mythic units become Epic after 4 player combats; Great (Wukong) grows his weapon every 3 casts. See traits.
- Artifacts expanded: 33 artifact items in the data.
