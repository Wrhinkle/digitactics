## Set mechanics

- Shadow Items: Shadow components appear on every carousel after the first and in the Armory. One Shadow component plus any component builds a Shadow item: stronger than the base item with a drawback (for example Sacrificial Infinity Edge damages the holder on crit). [11.9 notes]
- Carousels in Set 5 contained 2 Shadow items 70%, 3 25%, 4 5%. [wiki Carousel]
- The Armory debuted: a pop-up free pick of one item from a few options. [11.9 notes]
- Hyper Roll launched as TFT's first Lab mode. [11.9 notes]

## Economy and system changes

- Mana from damage taken: 6% pre-mitigation changed to 1% pre-mitigation + 7% post-mitigation. [11.9 notes]
- Units re-evaluate chase: move 1 hex only if that puts the target in range, else retarget. Assassins always jump first. [11.9 notes]
- Conventions: range measured in hexes, Spell Power renamed Ability Power, all physical damage can crit and be dodged. Shop cards of units you own glow. [11.9 notes]
- Odds: L7 19/35/30/15/1, L8 15/20/35/25/5. [11.9 notes]
- Component stat deflation: BF Sword 15 to 10 AD, Bow 15% to 10% AS, Rod 15 to 10 AP, Vest 25 to 20, Cloak 25 to 20, Belt 200 to 150 HP, Gloves 10% to 5% crit. [11.9 notes]
- New tiebreaker: "won a round more recently". Shorter carousel and early-round timers. [11.9 notes]
