## Set mechanics

- Hextech Augments introduced: pick one of three augments at 1-4, 3-3 and 4-5 (moved to 2-1, 3-2, 4-2 in Set 7). Three tiers (Silver, Gold, Prismatic); all players see the same tier at the same time. Augments appear on the Hexcore, visible to everyone. [11.22 notes]
- Double Up (2v2 co-op mode) followed in 11.23. [11.22 notes]
- Socialite trait marks hexes on the board that buff the unit standing on them. Mercenary uses a dice-based loot system. [CDragon trait text, 11.22 notes]
- Wiki notes a level 11 shop odds row exists only in Set 6 through Level Up / High End Shopping augments: 1/2/12/50/35. [wiki Champion]

## Economy and system changes

- Item reworks for the set: Archangel's Staff (+25 AP every 4 s), Banshee's Claw (row shield blocking first ability up to 600), Bramble Vest grants 80 armor, Deathblade flat 50/75/100 AD by star, Quicksilver adds 20% AS, Runaan's +10 AD and no on-hit. [11.22 notes]
- Neeko's Help drop rate from blue and gold orbs reduced slightly. [11.22 notes]
- Hyper Roll became a permanent mode; starting gold 8 to 2; total gold 85 to 107. [11.22 notes]

## Traits

### Academy

Academics have bonus Attack Damage and Ability Power. They also learn from their allies, gaining an additional bonus whenever an ally casts their Ability.

| Units | Effect |
|---|---|
| 2-3 | 18 Attack Damage and Ability Power; 3 from allies' casts |
| 4-5 | 40 Attack Damage and Ability Power; 5 from allies' casts |
| 6-7 | 50 Attack Damage and Ability Power; 10 from allies' casts |
| 8 | 70 Attack Damage and Ability Power; 15 from allies' casts |

### Arcanist

Arcanists increase the Ability Power of your team.

| Units | Effect |
|---|---|
| 2-3 | Your team gains 20 Ability Power |
| 4-5 | Your team gains 20 Ability Power, Arcanists gain an additional 40 |
| 6-7 | Your team gains 50 Ability Power, Arcanists gain an additional 50 |
| 8 | Your team gains 145 Ability Power |
