## Set mechanics

- Over 80 new augments added; Academy and Imperial augments retired; trait augments rebalanced. [12.4 notes]
- Opponent matchup tracker added to the UI (icon on opponents you have not fought this stage). Match history shows augments. [12.4 notes]

## Economy and system changes

## Traits

### Arcanist

Arcanists increase the Ability Power of your team.

| Units | Effect |
|---|---|
| 2-3 | Your team gains 20 Ability Power |
| 4-5 | Your team gains 20 Ability Power, Arcanists gain an additional 30 |
| 6-7 | Your team gains 50 Ability Power, Arcanists gain an additional 50 |
| 8 | Your team gains 150 Ability Power |

### Assassin

Innate: When combat starts, Assassins leap to the enemy backline. Assassins' Abilities can critically strike and they gain bonus Critical Strike Chance and bonus Critical Strike Damage.

| Units | Effect |
|---|---|
| 2-3 | +10% Crit Chance and +20% Crit Damage |
| 4-5 | +30% Crit Chance and +40% Crit Damage |
| 6 | +60% Crit Chance and +75% Crit Damage |

| Units | Effect |
|---|---|
| 2-3 | 80 Armor, 200 shield |
| 4-5 | 160 Armor, 400 shield |
| 6-7 | 250 Armor, 800 shield |
| 8 | 450 Armor, 1800 shield |
