## Set mechanics

- Galaxies continued with a rotation: from 10.13 one galaxy added and one removed per patch. Lilac Nebula removed in 10.12. [10.12 notes, wiki Galaxy]
- Galaxies added during 3.5: Binary Star (10.13, champions limited to two items), Dwarf Planet (10.14, nearest and farthest rows impassable), Plunder Planet (10.15, champions may drop coins), Salvage World (10.16, selling a champion breaks its completed items into components), Manatee's Delight (10.17, free Spatula), Big Bang (10.18, free reroll per round, orbs from all monsters, Force of Nature at stage 3). [wiki Galaxy]
- Galaxy odds 10.12: Normal 20%, each other galaxy 10%. 10.18: Big Bang 20%, others and Normal 10%. [wiki Galaxy]
- Bard (Astro) spawns a meep on the bench that sells for 1 XP. [10.12 notes]

## Economy and system changes

- Tier 3 champion pool: 16 to 18 copies. [10.12 notes]
- Sell value: 1-star and 1-cost units sell for full value; all others sell for 1 less than full value (2-star 3-cost: 5 to 8; 3-star 1-cost: 5 to 9). [10.12 notes]
- Odds: L4 55/30/15/0/0, L9 10/15/30/30/15. [10.12 notes]
- Neeko's Help always works even if no copies remain in the pool; the extra copy does not return to the pool. [10.12 notes]
