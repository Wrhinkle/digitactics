## Set mechanics and system changes

- Headliners: a Headliner is sold as a 2-star unit for the price of three copies (a 3-cost Headliner costs 9 gold). One of its traits is marked and counts double, and it gains a unique Headliner bonus. If you have no Headliner, one is always in your shop; if you have one, a Headliner appears every third shop. Riot described it as an evolution of the Set 4 Chosen mechanic.
- Legends were removed (no legends list in the Set 10 data).
- Portals stayed but are generic rule changes instead of regions (33 rows below).
- Unique single-champion traits: ILLBEATS (Illaoi), Mixmaster (Sona chooses a mode that changes her attacks and ability), Maestro (Jhin attacks at a fixed pace and converts bonus attack speed into AD), Wildcard (Kayn becomes Shadow Assassin after a won player combat or Rhaast after a loss, and pays 5 gold or 2 player health per 2 champion kills by form).
- Remix Rumble returned as a limited revival ("Remix Rumble" revival, May 14 to July 29, 2025, TFT patch 14.4) with new augments and an event pass. CDragon keeps that revival as TFTSet10 in patches 15.10 to 15.23 (84 units); this file documents the original 2023-24 set.
