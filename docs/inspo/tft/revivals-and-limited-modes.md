# TFT revivals and limited modes (Set 9 era onward)

Short note only. These modes reuse older sets; they are not documented champion by champion here.

Sources: invenglobal.com, esports.gg, oneesports.gg and ggrecon.com coverage of each revival; Riot dev post "Dev Double Up: Tocker's Trials & New Revival Ladder"; CommunityDragon `en_us.json` set lists checked across client versions 13.11 to 16.20.

| Mode | Based on | Dates | Patch | CDragon set data |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Set 3.5 Revival: Galaxies, "Return to the Stars" | Set 3.5 Galaxies | January 24, 2024 to February 20, 2024 | 14.2 to 14.3 | `TFTSet3` (appears from client 14.2) |
| Revival: Reckoning, "Dawn of Heroes" | Set 5 / 5.5 | September 25, 2024 to November 4, 2024 | 14.19 to 14.21 | `TFTSet5`, `TFTSet5_Stage2` (from client 14.19) |
| Revival: Fates, "Festival of Beasts" | Set 4 / 4.5 | from January 23, 2025 | TFT 13.4 | `TFTSet4`, `TFTSet4_Act2` (from client 15.2) |
| Revival: Remix Rumble | Set 10 | May 14, 2025 to July 29, 2025 | TFT 14.4 | `TFTSet10` (84 units, from client 15.10) |
| Revival: Dragonlands, "Uncharted Realms" | Set 7 / 7.5 | October 22, 2025 to December 2, 2025 | 15.21 | `TFTSet7`, `TFTSet7_Stage2` (from client 15.21) |

Notes:

- A revival usually lasts about two patch cycles and has its own progression and ranked ladder separate from the main set.
- CDragon also carries mode variants for current sets: `_PAIRS` (Double Up), `_TURBO` (Hyper Roll), `_PVEMODE` (PvE mode, Tocker's Trials per Riot's dev post) for Sets 13 to 17, and `TFTSet16_CarouselOfChaos`.
- Event mutators also exist in CDragon without a confirmed public name in the data: `TFTEvent_SF` (from 13.14), `TFTEvent_FF` (13.18 to 13.24), `TFTEvent_CT` / `TFTSetEventCT` (from 14.1), `TFTSetEvent5YR` (14.13 to 15.21, and again from 16.12), `TFTSetEventPM` (15.19 to 15.23), `TFTSetEventER` (from 16.14), `TFTSet7_Stage2_CT` (from 16.10). Names unknown; not documented here.
- `TFTSet13_Evolved` and `TFTSet13_Evolved_MacaoMode` exist from client 14.24; purpose not confirmed.
