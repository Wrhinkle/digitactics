## Set mechanics

- Glitched Out Carousels: always on stage 2, and may appear again between stages 3 and 6 (most games two or fewer, some three). Later ones have stronger glitches; contents include Loaded Dice, eggs, anvils, components, duplicators, Tome of Traits. In Hyper Roll they replace Armories. [13.6 notes]
- Items now bounce off the carousel champion when you return to your board. [13.6 notes]
- Threat Hero Augment limit: max tailored Threat offers per slot = active Threat level + 1; 2-1 stays fully random. [13.6 notes]
- Several Hero Augments reworked (e.g. Annie Reflector Shield to Pyromania). [13.6 notes]
