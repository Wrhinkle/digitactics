## Set mechanics and system changes

- Unlockable champions: 40 of the set's 100 champions must be unlocked during a game by meeting a condition (for example, a number of star levels of one trait, specific items, or player level). Once unlocked, a copy is guaranteed in the next shop (rightmost slot), and the unit stays unlocked for that game. Unlocked units you do not buy appear less often. Unlock conditions are listed in the champion table.
- 7-cost champions were introduced (Baron Nashor, Aurelion Sol, Brock, Sylas, Ryze, Zaahen per MetaTFT). All must be unlocked; they behave like 5-costs but are ranked higher.
- Regions return as origins, with unique single-champion traits for many legendary units.
- Bilgewater, Ixtal and Explorer quests use set-specific items (quests and black-market goods appear in the item list).
