## Set mechanics

- Galaxies: each game rolls one galaxy that changes the rules for every player (launched in 10.7). Galaxies at launch included Normal, Binary Star, Galactic Armory, Lilac Nebula, Littler Little Legends, Medium Legends, Star Cluster, Superdense, Trade Sector, Treasure Trove, The Neekoverse. [wiki Galaxy]
- Examples: Galactic Armory (everyone starts with the same items), Lilac Nebula (first carousel all 4-costs), Star Cluster (2-star units on carousel), Trade Sector (one free reroll per round), Treasure Trove (all minions/monsters drop orbs), Superdense (Force of Nature at 3-1). [wiki Galaxy]
- Mech-Pilot trait: three Mech-Pilots merge into one Super Mech during combat. [CDragon trait text]
- Space Pirate trait drops gold and items on kills. [CDragon]
- New items: Chalice of Favor (replaced Hush), Shroud of Stillness (replaced Iceborn Gauntlet), Zz'Rot Portal (replaced Titanic Hydra). [10.6 notes]

## Economy and system changes

- Passive income by round from 1-2: 2-2-3-4-5-5-5 (was 3-5-4-5-5-5). No selling champions during carousel. [10.6 notes]
- Streak gold: 2 = 1g, 3 = 2g, 4+ = 3g, paid during PvE rounds too. (10.8 changed to 2-3 = 1g, 4 = 2g, 5+ = 3g.) [10.6 notes, wiki Gold]
- Shop odds at launch: L3 70/30/0/0/0, L5 35/40/20/5/0, L6 20/35/35/10/0, L7 14/30/40/15/1, L8 13/20/35/25/7, L9 10/15/25/35/15. [10.6 notes]
- Player damage: stage base 0/3/4/5/10/15/20 plus 1 per surviving enemy unit (was star/cost based, 1-8). [10.6 notes]
- Mana per attack: flat 10 for all star levels. Movement speed 550 to 500. [10.6 notes]
- First carousel is all 1-costs. Spatulas can appear on carousels again. [10.6 notes]
- Grievous Wounds reduced healing 80% to 50%. Disarm no longer stops casts. [10.6 notes]
